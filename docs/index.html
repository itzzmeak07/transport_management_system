ecoFreshTrack

Product design preview

How the FreshTrack transport management system will look: the admin web panel and the Android app for vehicle owners, drivers and dealers. Every screen uses one sample trip: truck MH13-AB-1234 leaves Solapur APMC with goods for three dealers (Hyderabad → Vijayawada → Visakhapatnam) and delivers them in the order the admin set.

Design mockups for discussion. Names, numbers and maps are illustrative sample data.

Style Guide & Overview

Brand, colours, type, stop markers, components, sample trip, and the key screens at a glance.

Admin Dashboard

The dispatcher's all-day screen: KPIs, live map, needs-attention list, active trips.

Dealer App & Tracking Link
